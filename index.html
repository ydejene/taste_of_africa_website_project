Regional Spotlight

West Africa

Known for bold aromatics, fiery scotch bonnet peppers, fermented seasoning pastes like iru and dawadawa, and iconic tomato-rich celebration stews paired with pounded yam, fufu, and jollof rice.

Staples: Yam, cassava, plantain, scotch bonnet, iru, palm oil, tomatoes.

Regional Spotlight

North & Northeast Africa

Rooted in ancient Nile agricultural civilizations, celebrating slow-simmered legumes like fava beans and lentils, toasted cumin, fresh mint, coriander, dates, and rustic hearth-baked flatbreads.

Staples: Fava beans, cumin, coriander, sesame oil, garlic, flatbreads, fenugreek.

Regional Spotlight

East Africa & Islands

From the Ethiopian and Eritrean highlands with teff injera and complex berbere blends to Madagascar’s coastal curries enriched with wild cloves, ginger, vanilla, and leafy paracres greens.

Staples: Teff, berbere, niter kibbeh, cloves, ginger, anamalao, coconut.

Ingredient Spotlight

The Revival of Ancient African Grains and Forest Spices

Centuries before industrial agriculture, cooks across the Sahel and Horn of Africa relied on drought-resilient grains like fonio, sorghum, and iron-rich teff. Today, visionary chefs and farmers are re-centering these ancestral grains alongside forest botanicals such as grains of paradise and wild selim pepper to build biodiverse culinary futures.

Culinary Craft

The Art of the Slow Fire: Secrets of the Communal Pot

Across the continent, meaningful cooking is never rushed. From the clay pot tagines of the Maghreb to the cast-iron potjies of the south, labor-intensive onion caramelization and gentle simmering allow deep flavor matrices to develop, creating stews intended for shared communal eating with bare hands and flatbreads.

Living Ritual

Buna Tetu: Hospitality and Stillness in the Coffee Ceremony

In Ethiopia and Eritrea, preparing coffee is a sacred social rhythm. The ritual involves roasting raw green beans over glowing charcoal embers, wafting the aromatic smoke toward honored guests, and serving coffee in three mindful rounds known as Abol, Tona, and Baraka to deepen neighborhood bonds.
